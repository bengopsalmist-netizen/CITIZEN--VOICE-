Citizen Voice

Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 23, 2026

Citizen Voice is a civic communication platform designed to help citizens share questions, concerns, and feedback with public offices.

This Privacy Policy explains how Citizen Voice collects, uses, stores, and protects information submitted through the platform.

1. Information We Collect

When you use Citizen Voice, you may provide information such as:

  • Name
  • County
  • Constituency
  • Ward
  • Location
  • Sub-location
  • Public office concerned
  • Issue category
  • Description of your question or concern

Authorized officials and administrators may also provide account information required to access the official or administrative areas of the platform.

2. How We Use Information

Information submitted through Citizen Voice may be used to:

  • Receive and organize citizens' questions and concerns.
  • Help authorized public offices review community feedback.
  • Identify the location and category of reported concerns.
  • Publish official announcements through the platform.
  • Maintain and improve the Citizen Voice service.
  • Protect the security and proper operation of the platform.

Citizen Voice does not guarantee that every submitted concern will receive a response or action from a public office.

3. Firebase and Data Storage

Citizen Voice uses Firebase services to support authentication, data storage, and application functionality.

Information submitted through the platform may therefore be stored and processed using Firebase infrastructure.

4. Public Offices and Authorized Users

Citizen Voice is designed to allow authorized public offices to review relevant citizen submissions and publish announcements.

Access to official and administrative areas is restricted to authorized users.

Citizens do not have access to the administrative dashboard.

5. Information Sharing

Citizen Voice does not sell users' personal information.

Information may be made available to authorized users of the relevant public office where necessary to process or review a citizen submission.

Information may also be disclosed where required by applicable law or where necessary to protect the security and integrity of the service.

6. Data Security

Reasonable measures are used to protect information stored through Citizen Voice.

However, no internet-based service can guarantee absolute security of information.

Users should avoid submitting passwords, financial information, or other highly sensitive information in the description of a question or concern.

7. Data Retention

Citizen Voice may retain submitted information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the service, maintain records, resolve issues, comply with legal obligations, and protect the platform.

Retention periods may vary depending on the type of information and its purpose.

8. Children's Privacy

Citizen Voice is intended for general civic communication and is not specifically directed at children.

We do not knowingly request unnecessary personal information from children.

9. Third-Party Services

Citizen Voice uses third-party services, including Firebase, to provide certain technical functions.

These services may process information according to their own privacy policies and applicable terms.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time.

When changes are made, the updated version will be published through the Citizen Voice website or application.
